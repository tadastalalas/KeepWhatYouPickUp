KWYPU v1.5.7.1 :
- Fixed a crash or the mod stopping to work after loading a save while already playing another one.

KWYPU v1.5.7.0 :
- Updated for game version 1.5.7. Game versions 1.5.7, 1.4.8 and 1.4.7 are in the same download.
- Mod Configuration Menu is no longer required. Without it, change the settings in Modules\KeepWhatYouPickUp\ModConfiguration.xml.
- "Enable Logging" was replaced by "Logging for debugging" in the new "Technical settings" group (default: off).
	It writes to Modules\KeepWhatYouPickUp\logs.
- Fixed broken shields and used-up javelins/ammo being removed from the hero's equipment after a mission; they now stay, as in vanilla.
	- Added the setting "Lose Broken Shields And Spent Throwing Weapons" (General, default: off) to lose them instead, for realism.
	- Also works for knocked-out or killed heroes.
- Picked-up weapons above the hero's skill now go to the party inventory instead of disappearing.
	- Military weapons picked up while wearing civilian gear go to the inventory instead of the civilian set.
	- Your own weapon, dropped for an item that cannot be equipped, no longer comes back twice.
	- AI heroes without a party keep such weapons equipped instead of losing them.
- Weapons falling from a knocked-out or killed hero no longer count as dropped by the player.
	- Settlement scenes no longer also leave a copy of that weapon on the ground for the next visit.
- Added on-screen messages:
	- On pickup: item needs more skill / can't be worn with civilian clothes / weapons picked up here are not kept.
	- On leaving: list of weapons kept and items moved to the inventory.
- "Stolen Equipment Chance" default lowered from 10% to 3% per day.
- Added translations for 12 languages (all settings and messages).
